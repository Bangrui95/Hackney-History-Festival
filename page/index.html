Hackney History Festival began in 2024 to celebrate the people, places and stories that have shaped the borough — and to keep them alive for future generations.

From centuries of change to the communities that call Hackney home today, its history lives on in its streets, buildings and people.

Hackney is famous for its street art, which can be seen all over the area. The borough is also known for its thriving music scene, and has produced a number of famous musicians and artists over the years. Hackney is also home to a number of beautiful parks and green spaces, including Victoria Park and Hackney Marshes.
